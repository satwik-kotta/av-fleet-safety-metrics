How traffic on the Hollywood Freeway changes as the morning rush builds

NGSIM US-101: every vehicle on a 640 m stretch of US-101 in Los Angeles, tracked ten times a second from 7:50 to 8:35 on June 15, 2005. This page measures lane changes, hard braking and near misses, groups lane changes into behaviors, and tests a model that predicts a lane change 3 seconds ahead as traffic slows.
